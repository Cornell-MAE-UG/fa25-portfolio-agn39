###### **Reflection of Homework 4**

I believe that Problem Set 4 was the most valuable homework that I have completed this semseter. The content of the problems themselves seem very relevant to problems that could be encountered in professional life, rather than just anlayzing simple rods or chips. For example, the solar array question integrated almost all types of heat transfer methods (conduction, convection, radiation, and generation). I think that was usefull in helping me tackle complicated problems that have multiple components to them. I also thought it was beneficial learning how device efficiency can depend on heat transfer. Furthermore, this problem set introduced the concept of fins, which I think helped me think of different ways geometry can affect heat transfer and cooling techniques.

Furthermore, I enjoyed how in this problem set, I was able to apply my knowledge of thermal resitance to solve problems. I think that it helped me see different ways of solveing heat transfer problems, rather than just thinking of energy balances. In that sense, I also think it helped me discern whether or not I could use differnt methods to solve problems based on the information I am given and the information I need to find. Overall, I enjoyed completing this homework the most, and I think it's been the most beneficial at helping me further understand the course content.
